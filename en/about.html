BUTTON is a branding PR agency that delivers total branding and marketing solutions and activities for artists, companies and creative works.

PHILOSOPHY

The first button

Branding is doing up the first button right

To open a door to the world, the first thing to do is press the button. The name BUTTON carries two meanings.

The first is the first button on a garment. If it is done up wrong, the rest never sits right, however carefully made. Brands are the same: how a brand is first introduced to the world shapes every impression that follows.

The second is the button that opens a door. Great artists, companies and works still need a door that opens onto their audience; until it opens, the story does not begin. BUTTON opens that door.
